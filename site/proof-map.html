The argument in dimension 126

Does there exist a closed smooth stably framed 126-manifold of Kervaire invariant one?

The invariant takes values in {0, 1}; the goal is to realize the value 1. Browder’s criterion translates this geometric question into a precise survival question in the sphere Adams spectral sequence.
